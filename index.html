The 10-minute plan

If you only do three things today, do these, in this order.

Minutes 1–3

Turn on 2FA for your main email

This one step limits the damage from almost every other weak point on this list.

Minutes 4–7

Review app permissions

Revoke camera, mic, and location access from anything that doesn't clearly need it.

Minutes 8–10

Install a free password manager

Start with your email and banking logins — you can migrate the rest over time.
